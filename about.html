Our story & commitment

Started in a rented kitchen, 2019.

Veloura began with a single serum, blended in small batches on weekends and shared at our neighborhood farmer's market stall.

Frustrated by cosmetic brands that masked filler petroleum oils with synthetic fragrances, we decided to craft skincare that works in harmony with human biology: nutrient-dense botanicals, cold-pressed oils, and zero non-functional stabilizers.

We still formulate everything with the same uncompromising ethos: slow runs, heavy glass vessels designed to be refilled forever, and honest ingredient declarations in plain language.
