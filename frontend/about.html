Our Story & Philosophy

Started in a rented kitchen, 2019.

Veloura began with a single serum, blended in small batches on weekend mornings and shared by hand at our neighborhood farmer's market stall.

Frustrated by cosmetic corporations that mask inexpensive petroleum filler oils with synthetic fragrances, we decided to craft skincare that works in harmony with human biology: nutrient-dense cold-pressed botanicals, biocompatible squalane, and zero non-functional stabilizers.

We still formulate every batch with the same uncompromising slow-beauty ethos: small artisanal runs, heavy Italian flint glass designed to be refilled forever, and honest ingredient declarations in plain, understandable language.

The Veloura Code

Three commitments in every bottle

No compromises on sourcing, purity, or environmental impact.

✦

Cold-Pressed Potency

We never heat-treat our active botanical oils. Cold hydraulic pressing retains vital polyphenol compounds, tocopherols, and bio-active nutrients.

✦

Biocompatible Lipids

Every molecule is selected to closely mimic human sebum chemistry. Skincare that sinks deep within seconds without coating pores in greasy film.

✦

Never Discard Glass

Keep your heavy glass vessel on your vanity indefinitely. Our lightweight refill pods reduce freight shipping emissions by 86% per shipment.
